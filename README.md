## Application Landing Page

AutoStockIQ connects Kwena ka Bobedi with South African schools through a unified stationery supply platform, providing dedicated portal entry points for staff and schools to ensure clear security and role separation.

</p>

### Key Landing Page Features:
* **Dual Portal Architecture:** Separate sign-in experiences for corporate staff (`@autostockiq.co.za`) and independent school administrators.
* **Streamlined Navigation:** Quick access links to Home, Staff Portal, School Portal, and Privacy policies.
* **Clear Role Guidance:** Informative breakdown explaining the rationale behind split portals to prevent mistaken login attempts.
